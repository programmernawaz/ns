Our Services

Nawal Salem Saeed's Law Firm in Dubai announces the opening of private notary services, which include but not limited to:

  1. Drafting or notarizing contracts or documents.

  2. Authenticating signatures on contracts or documents.

  3. Drafting or notarizing general or special powers of attorney.

  4. Confirming the date of the contract or document.

  5. Notarizing any translations on a contract or document.

  6. Notarizing legal notices.

  7. Putting the executive formula on the document

  8. Authenticating affidavits sworn on oath.

  9. Providing a true copy of the contract.

  10. Issuing a certificate from the notary's records or files.

  11. Any other authorities entrusted to the private notary under any other law.
    Please note that these services are offered by the law firm and its private notary in compliance with applicable laws.
