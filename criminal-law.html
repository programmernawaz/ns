CRIMINAL LAW


Criminal law is the area that deals with illegal action; it places more emphasis on punitive and rehabilitative judgements than it does on purely compensatory ones. Criminal law is a component of the federal system based on Islamic Sharia law of the United Arab Emirates and Dubai. The death sentence or the physical penalty may be applied as a deterrence to criminal activity in extreme circumstances. In order to prosecute a criminal case, there must be proof of both criminal activity and criminal intent. This is known as actus reus, or the guilty act, and mens rea, or the guilty mind.
